# Data-Analysis-Dashboard
Interactive Excel dashboard analysing a year of online fashion sales across marketplaces. Built with pivot tables, charts and slicers (Month, Channel, Category). Covers monthly sales vs orders, gender split, channel share, age-gender orders, top 5 states and order status. Women drive 64% of sales; Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart bring ~80% of orders.
1. How do monthly sales and order volume change throughout the year?
2. Which month had the highest and lowest sales?
3. What share of total sales comes from men and from women?
4. Which sales channel (Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, Meesho, Ajio, Nalli) brings in the most orders?
5. Which age group and gender place the most orders?
6. Which top 5 states generate the highest sales?
7. What percentage of orders are delivered successfully?
8. What are the cancellation, refund, and return rates?
9. Which product category (kurta, saree, blouse, set, etc.) sells the most?
10. How does the sales performance of each category differ across channels and months?
